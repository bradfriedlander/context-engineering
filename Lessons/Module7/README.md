# **Module 7: The Future of Context**

This capstone module looks to the future, exploring the cutting-edge of context engineering. We will cover multi-modal models that understand more than just text, the evolving landscape of new protocols and standards, and the business and ethical considerations of building advanced AI systems. We conclude with a unifying architectural theory that ties all the concepts of the course together.
